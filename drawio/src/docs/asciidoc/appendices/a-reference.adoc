== API reference

A summary of the API by package, for looking things up.
The Javadoc of the module is the complete reference.

=== `org.nasdanika.sdk.runtime.drawio`

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
| Type | What it is for

| `Document`
| The file. Static `load` methods for strings, readers, streams, files, URLs and URIs, `data:` URIs included; `loadFromPngMetadata` for PNG images; `loadFromSvg` for SVG images with the document in the `content` attribute; `create` for a new document. Instance methods: `getPages`, `createPage`, `getPageByName`, `getPageById`, `save`, `toHtml`, `toDataURI`, `embedInPng`, `getDocument(URI)` for linked documents, `getProperty` from the context.

| `Document.Context`
| What a document is loaded with: `openStream(URI)` for linked documents, `getProperty(name)` for placeholders, `filterProperty(element, name, value)`. Factories `fromPropertySource`, `fromURIHandler`, `fromURIConverter`; `from(...)`, `compose` and `reduce` to combine contexts.

| `Document.EvaluatingContext`
| A context that evaluates placeholders itself, for an expression language of your choice.

| `Page`
| A page: `getName`, `getId`, `getModel`, `getDocument`, `getTags`, `createTag`, `remove`.

| `Model`
| The page's settings: grid, grid size, background color and image, shadow, page view. `getRoot`.

| `Root`
| The root cell: `getLayers`, `createLayer`.

| `Layer`
| `getElements`, `createNode`, `createConnection(source, target)`.

| `Node`
| A shape, and a layer of its own. `getGeometry`, `getConnectionPoints`, `createConnectionPoint`, incoming and outgoing connections with and without connection points, a `NodeStyle`.

| `Connection`
| `getSource`, `getTarget` and their setters, source and target points, `getPoints`, label `getOffset`, `createNode` for labels, a `ConnectionStyle`.

| `ConnectionPoint`, `ConnectionPointSpec`
| A position relative to a node, `x` and `y` from 0 to 1, offsets `dx` and `dy` in pixels, and `perimeter`.

| `Element`
| The base of everything: `getURI`, `getMarker`, `getElement` for the DOM, `accept` visitors, `stream`, `getModelElementById`, `getModelElementByProperty`, `getModelElementsByProperty`.

| `ModelElement`
| The base of the root, layers, nodes and connections: `getId`, `getLabel`, `getTooltip`, `getLink`, `getStyle`, `getProperty`, `setProperty`, `getPropertyNames`, `getTags`, `isVisible`, `getParent`, `getModel`, `getPath`, `getPosition`, `getLinkTarget`, `isTargetLink`, `select`, `resolve`, `resolveURI`, `getEnumarateValue`, `remove`.

| `ConnectionBase`
| `PARENT`, `SOURCE` or `TARGET`: the logical parent of connections for visitors, streams and URIs.

| `Geometry`, `Rectangle`, `Point`, `PointList`
| Positions and sizes, the alternate bounds of a collapsed container, and the points of a connection.

| `Tag`, `Marker`, `LinkTarget`, `Connectable`, `LayerElement`
| As in <<document-model>>.
|===

=== `org.nasdanika.sdk.runtime.drawio.style`

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
| Type | Typed methods, on top of `Map<String, String>`

| `Style`
| `opacity`, `rounded`, `shadow`, `enumerate`, `enumerateValue`

| `LineStyle`
| `color` (`strokeColor`), `width` (`strokeWidth`), `dashed`

| `NodeStyle`
| `backgroundColor` (`fillColor`), `shape`, `align`, `verticalAlign`, `fontSize`, `fontColor`, `fontStyle`, `labelBackgroundColor`, `labelBorderColor`, `rotation`, `container`, `collapsible`

| `ConnectionStyle`
| `edgeStyle`, `startArrow`, `endArrow`, `startFill`, `endFill`, `jump`

| `Arrow`, `Jump`
| The common values of the arrow and line jump keys.
|===

=== `org.nasdanika.sdk.runtime.drawio.comparators`

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
| Comparator | Order

| `LabelModelElementComparator`
| By label text, HTML removed, empty labels last.

| `PropertyModelElementComparator`
| By the value of a property, empty values last.

| `PositionModelElementComparator`
| By position in the parent: the order of the file.

| `FlowComparator`
| By the number of steps along connections from one element to the other, optionally only along connections that match a predicate. Not total.

| `EnumerateComparator`
| By Draw.io enumeration values, compared as dotted paths of numbers and words. Not total.

| `AngularNodeComparator`
| By angle around a hub node, counterclockwise from a base angle in radians, twelve o'clock by default.

| `CartesianNodeComparator`
| By columns and rows, in one of eight directions.

| `Comparators`
| Keys for the orders above, such as `label`, `property`, `flow`, `clockwise`, `right-down`, `position`, `enumerate`, and their reverses.
|===

=== `org.nasdanika.sdk.runtime.drawio.util`

`Util.getConnectableNode` returns the node of a connection point, or the node itself.
`Util.withLinkTargets` wraps a visitor so that it visits link targets; `Util.traverser` wraps one so that it visits connected elements, parents and link targets.
`Util.loadStyle` parses a style string into a read-only map.
`DelimitedStringMap` and the `AbstractSplitJoin` collections are the live views behind styles and tags, available for attributes of your own in the same formats.
`ConfigurationException` is thrown for links and selectors that cannot be resolved, with the marker of the element at fault.

ifdef::editor-notes[]
[IMPORTANT,caption="Editor's note"]
====
`Util.traverser` has two overloads. The one that takes a `BiFunction` visits the target of a connection twice and never its source: it calls `getConnectableNode(connection.getTarget())` for both. The `Consumer` overload is correct. The `package-info.java` of the module links to the documentation of Nasdanika core, and the Javadoc of `Document.Context.getVariables` still mentions `$spel` magic properties, which the module no longer has; `getVariables` itself is not used by the module. The `impl` and `style.impl` packages are exported, which makes the implementation classes part of the API.
====
endif::[]

=== `org.nasdanika.sdk.runtime.drawio.playwright`

In the module `drawio-playwright` (<<rendering>>).

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
| Type | What it is for

| `Rendering`
| Renders pages with Draw.io's viewer in a browser: `png` and `svg` for a document's first page, a page, or pages with a file name function and an optional `PageBody`; `video` for frames; `inViewer` for full control; `fileNames(extension)`. A record with `with` methods; `DEFAULT`.

| `Rendering.PageBody`
| What to do with a page once the viewer shows it: `run(viewer, page)`.

| `Viewer`
| A document open in the viewer: `page`, `document`, `show`, `click` by ID or element, `png`, `svg`, `session`. The viewer is the browser's global variable `nasdanikaViewer`.

| `Frame`
| A page of a video, its duration and an optional `PageBody`.

| `Pages`
| Pages to render: `byId`, `byName`, `all`.
|===

=== Selectors

[cols="2,4",options="header"]
|===
| Selector | Selects

| `..` | The parent.
| `child`, `child[id]`, `child[name=value]` | Children, all or filtered.
| `link-target` | What the link points to: an element, or the root of a page.
| `incoming-connection`, `outgoing-connection`, with `[id]` or `[name=value]` | A node's connections, including those of its connection points.
| `source`, `target` | A connection's ends.
| `segment/segment/...` | A path. As a property name, the last segment is the property.
|===

=== Links

[cols="3,3",options="header"]
|===
| Link | Example

| `data:page/id,[<document>#]<page ID>` | `data:page/id,containers`
| `data:page/name,[<document>#]<page name>` | `data:page/name,overview.drawio#System+Context`
| `data:element/id,[[<document>#]<page>/]<element ID>` | `data:element/id,id,system-context/ibs`
| `data:element/name,[[<document>#]<page>/]<label>` | `data:element/name,name,Page+2/Linked`
|===

In element links, `<page>` is `id,<page ID>` or `name,<page name>`.
Names and labels are URL-encoded.
