[#landscape]
== The landscape

*Observability platforms* are built for the firehose: ingestion of high cardinality, sampling, short retention, questions about health now.
This model is not a competitor and would be a poor one.
It is for the small fraction of signals worth keeping, with the same instrumentation and a different exporter.

*Observability tools for language models and agents* keep traces of prompts and tool calls per session, which refer to names in someone else's service.
Here a span lands next to the models the agent worked on, and its changes are in it.

*OpenTelemetry itself* says how signals travel; collectors and backends say where they go.
Neither says what signals mean against a model of the system.
This model is a destination that stays faithful to OpenTelemetry at its boundary.

*Audit logs and W3C PROV.*
Audit logs have the instinct to retain, but no types and no joins.
PROV has the right concepts, activity, agent and entity, as a separate vocabulary to adopt.
Here provenance is part of the same models.

*The status quo* is grep over archives of logs, a wiki page titled "why did this change", and the person who remembers.
The claim of this model is that the person who remembers can be a query.
