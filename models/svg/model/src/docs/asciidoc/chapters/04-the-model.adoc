[#the-model]
== The model

=== Classes and elements

Each class is an SVG element, named after it: `Rect` is `rect`, `G` is `g`, `TSpan` is `tspan`.

[cols="2,3,4",options="header"]
|===
| Class | Element | Its own attributes

| `Svg` | `svg` | `version`, `x`, `y`, `width`, `height`, `viewBox`, `preserveAspectRatio`
| `G` | `g` |
| `Defs` | `defs` |
| `A` | `a` | `href`, `target`
| `Marker` | `marker` | `viewBox`, `refX`, `refY`, `markerWidth`, `markerHeight`, `orient`, `markerUnits`
| `ClipPath` | `clipPath` | `clipPathUnits`
| `LinearGradient` | `linearGradient` | `x1`, `y1`, `x2`, `y2`, `gradientUnits`
| `Stop` | `stop` | `offset`, `stop-color`, `stop-opacity`
| `Rect` | `rect` | `x`, `y`, `width`, `height`, `rx`, `ry`
| `Circle` | `circle` | `cx`, `cy`, `r`
| `Ellipse` | `ellipse` | `cx`, `cy`, `rx`, `ry`
| `Line` | `line` | `x1`, `y1`, `x2`, `y2`
| `Polyline`, `Polygon` | `polyline`, `polygon` | `points`
| `Path` | `path` | `d`
| `Image` | `image` | `href`, `x`, `y`, `width`, `height`, `preserveAspectRatio`
| `Use` | `use` | `href`, `x`, `y`
| `Text`, `TSpan` | `text`, `tspan` | `x`, `y`, `dx`, `dy`
| `Title`, `Desc`, `Style` | `title`, `desc`, `style` | `value`, the content; `type` for `style`
|===

Feature names are the attribute names in camel case: `stroke-width` is `strokeWidth`.
Two are renamed: the `class` attribute is `styleClass`, and the `style` attribute is `styleAttribute`, because `getClass()` belongs to Java and `Style` is the element.

Coordinates and sizes of shapes are doubles.
Attributes that SVG allows to carry units, percentages or lists, such as `width` of `svg`, `x` of `text`, `stroke-width` and `points`, are strings, written as given.
An attribute that is not set is not written, and SVG's default applies.

=== The base classes

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
| Class | What it has

| `Element`, abstract | `id`, `styleClass`, `styleAttribute`, `transform`: what every element of the subset has.
| `Presentation`, interface | The presentation attributes: `fill`, `fillOpacity`, `stroke`, `strokeWidth`, `strokeOpacity`, `strokeDasharray`, `strokeLinecap`, `strokeLinejoin`, `opacity`, `fontFamily`, `fontSize`, `fontWeight`, `fontStyle`, `textAnchor`, `dominantBaseline`, `markerStart`, `markerMid`, `markerEnd`, `visibility`, `clipPath`. Presentation attributes are inherited in SVG, so setting them on a group sets them for its children.
| `Container`, abstract | Children, in `content`. Every structure element and every shape is a container, so a shape can have a `title`.
| `Shape`, abstract | The shapes.
| `TextContent`, abstract | Characters and spans, in `mixed`, and the position attributes of text.
| `DocumentRoot` | The document: the root `svg` element and the namespace prefixes. `SvgFormat` creates it.
|===

=== Children and text

A container's children are in a feature map, `content`, so that one sequence holds elements of every kind in drawing order.
Text's characters and spans are in another, `mixed`.
The model's operations work with them without the feature maps:

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
| Operation | Does
| `container.add(child)` | Appends a child and returns it, typed as it was given
| `container.children()` | The children, in order
| `text.addText(characters)` | Appends characters
| `text.addSpan(span)` | Appends a span and returns it
| `text.text()` | The characters of the text and its spans, in order
|===
