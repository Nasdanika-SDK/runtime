== Federated by design

There is no server and no central repository.
A vocabulary is files in Git, or an artifact published to Maven Central or npm, and packages reference each other by namespace URI.
A team owns its vocabulary and refers to another team's without coordinating a release, and gets what a catalog cannot give:

* *Branching.* A proposed change to a term, reviewed as a pull request, with a diff that shows which features moved.
* *History.* The same history the code has, with the same tools.
* *Ownership at the edges.* Nobody administers the whole graph.
* *Immutable snapshots.* What a term meant last year is an install, not an investigation.

=== Directly on the core model

Meta sits directly on the runtime's core model, and on nothing else.
What it needs from below is identity, documentation as structure and markers.
That is deliberate: Meta is the foundation of every package generated from it, and a browser application should not drag a governance model in to render a list of customers, nor an agent pay for vocabulary it does not use in its context window on every call.
Richer variants extend Meta, not the other way around.

=== The landscape

[cols="2,3,4",options="header"]
|===
| Camp | Examples | What it leaves undone
| Industry data models | Licensed enterprise data models | Content you license, not a vocabulary you own; thousands of entities
| Standards | schema.org, FHIR, FIBO, ISO 20022 | Good content, delivered as OWL files, PDFs and spreadsheets with little tooling. Sources for Meta, not rivals
| Data catalogs | Collibra, Alation, Atlan | Organized around where data lives; the meaning stays thin, and the graph is central and rented
| Semantic layers | dbt, Cube, LookML | Analytics only: metrics, dimensions and joins, with no ownership or lifecycle
| Ontology platforms | Palantir Foundry | Has the whole thing and keeps it: the ontology does not leave the platform
| Modeling tools | Sparx EA, erwin, MagicDraw | The desktop era, weak federation, and the output is a document
| The actual incumbent | Spreadsheets and wiki tables | Drift within weeks, cannot be queried, and are invisible to an agent
|===

What is different here is not the part about classes and features, which everybody has.
It is the combination: composition with the other models of the runtime, federation without a server, documentation as structure, and portability, a vocabulary reaching a browser and an agent's context as a typed package, without a JVM and without a platform.
