== One definition, many spellings

A class with named, typed, possibly multi-valued features is one of the most stable ideas in computing, and every ecosystem spells it its own way: Ecore, Java, XML Schema, JSON Schema, a database schema, TypeScript, a table on a wiki page, a spreadsheet.
The differences are real and small, and most of them are about the host language, not about the domain.

The cost is not the spelling.
It is that one definition, of a customer, an order or a service, lives in many formats at once and nothing relates them: a DDL script, a JSON Schema, a partner's XSD, a Java class, a wiki table and a few spreadsheets.
Each is a projection of a domain model that was never written down.
Meta is the place to write it down once, and project.

=== A published language

In the terms of Domain-Driven Design, Meta is a https://martinfowler.com/bliki/PublishedLanguage.html[Published Language]: a documented, shared vocabulary that bounded contexts translate into and out of.
It adds the part that was out of reach when the term was coined: the vocabulary is machine-checkable, because the types and the documentation are generated from it.

It is deliberately not a superset of any of its sources.
Whatever does not fit is dropped by design or carried in an annotation, and the loaders decide which (<<what-meta-leaves-out>>).

=== Publishing is the deliverable

A model that cannot be read by the people who own the vocabulary is a private notation.
A vocabulary published as a site, with a page per element, its documentation, its neighbors and a diagram of its neighborhood, is a glossary that cannot drift from the types, because both come from one source.
That is what a business stakeholder opens, and nobody has to hear the word "metamodel".

=== Agents need structure

An assistant reasoning over an organization's systems has to know what a customer is, what an account is, and how they relate.
Today that context is assembled by hand, per project, from whatever prose can be found.
An agent given a declared, versioned, owned vocabulary answers from it and cites it; an agent given a DDL script and a wiki export infers, and inference is not a chain of custody.

=== The incumbent is a spreadsheet

The competitor is not another modeling tool.
It is a spreadsheet and a wiki table, which win because they are free, everyone can use them, and they never ask anyone to adopt anything.
Meta treats them as inputs, not as enemies: the vocabulary is captured where it already lives, and projected from there [.planned]#PLANNED#.
