== Glossary

Connector::
A line between two shapes, or their ports: a connection of the diagram model.

Deck::
A presentation: an ordered set of slides with their masters.

Master::
A slide whose shapes appear on the slides that use it, such as a corporate template.

Notes::
The text for the speaker of a slide, not shown when presenting; here also a carrier of annotations.

Point::
A unit of length of 1/72 of an inch, the unit of the model's geometry.

Run::
A piece of text with one formatting, within a paragraph.

Semantic element::
An object of another model that a diagram element depicts, such as the system a box on a slide stands for.
