[#the-model]
== The model

[cols="1,4",options="header"]
|===
| Area | Classes
| Deck | `Presentation`, a diagram: `width` and `height` in points, `slides` and `masterSlides`
| Slides | `Slide`, a diagram: `master`, `hidden`, `notes`; `MasterSlide`, a slide; `Notes`, with `text`
| Shapes | `Shape`, an abstract diagram node: `order`, `rotation`, `flipHorizontal`, `flipVertical`; `GroupShape`, `ImageShape`, `MediaShape`
| Text | `TextShape`, with `paragraphs`; `Paragraph`, with `runs`, `indentLevel` and `alignment`; `TextRun`, with `text`, `fontFamily`, `fontSize`, `bold`, `italic`, `underline`, `color` and `hyperlink`
| Tables | `TableShape`, with `rows`; `Row`, with `cells` and a `height`; `Cell`, with `paragraphs`, `columnSpan` and `rowSpan`
| Connectors | `ConnectorShape`, a diagram connection: `source`, `target`, `waypoints`
|===

=== What comes from the diagram model

Every element of a deck but the text, the tables' rows and cells, and the notes is a diagram element, so it has:

* `id` and `name`, documentation, markers that record where it came from, an `icon` and `uris`, from the runtime's core model;
* `attributes`, a map of strings, for what a format carries and the model does not;
* `semanticElements`, the objects of other models that it depicts;
* `children`, the elements it contains, keyed by `id`.

A shape has `bounds`, relative to its parent, and `ports`; connectable elements know their `incomingConnections` and `outgoingConnections`, kept in step with the connections' `source` and `target`.

=== Presentation

A `Presentation` is a diagram whose children are its slides and masters.
`slides` and `masterSlides` are derived from `children`: the children that are slides, and the children that are masters.
They are views, and changes to them write through to `children`.

=== Slide and MasterSlide

A `Slide` has its `master`, `hidden` for slides kept but not presented, and `notes`.
Its shapes and connectors are its children.
A `MasterSlide` is a slide, whose shapes appear on the slides that use it.

=== Shapes

`Shape` is abstract.
`order` is the order of drawing, `rotation` is in degrees, and the flips are booleans.
A `GroupShape` groups the shapes that are its children; an `ImageShape` and a `MediaShape` have a `location`, a URI resolved against the resource or the name of an embedded part, and a `contentType`.

=== Text and tables

A `TextShape` contains paragraphs; a `Paragraph` contains runs, with an `indentLevel` for bullets and an `alignment`.
A `TextRun` is text with the same formatting: `fontFamily`, `fontSize` in points, `bold`, `italic`, `underline`, a `color` as `#RRGGBB`, and a `hyperlink`, which is what makes a generated deck navigable.

A `TableShape` contains rows of cells, and a `Cell` contains paragraphs, as a text shape does, and spans columns and rows.

=== Connectors

A `ConnectorShape` is a diagram connection, with a `source` and a `target`, which can be shapes or their ports, and `waypoints` that keep its route.
