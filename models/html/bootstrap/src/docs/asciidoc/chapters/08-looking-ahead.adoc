== Looking ahead

Everything in this chapter is [.planned]#PLANNED#.

=== Documents

A document model as a pivot: structure in the model, text in a named format.
Markdown comes in through a ported model of its syntax, and documents go out as AsciiDoc, and as Bootstrap pages through the models of this book.

=== Documentation sites for models

A page per element of any model built on the runtime's core model: its documentation, its neighbors, links to where it came from, and a diagram of its neighborhood, generated as SVG, with every shape a link.
The navigation trees of <<navigation-trees>> are their side panels.

=== Pages that work offline

The stylesheets and scripts of Bootstrap, Bootswatch and Bootstrap Icons copied into the site, with the pages pointing at the copies, for offline use and closed networks.

=== The polymorphic UI

Views chosen by the class of the element they show and the format asked for, assembled into pages and applications, static with this book's models and interactive with React's.
