== Components

The components are in four packages: content, navigation and overlays under `components`, and forms.

=== Content

The card's parts are features, not children in a particular order: the header, the image with its position, the bodies with their titles, texts and links.
The structure writes them in Bootstrap's order.
The accordion's items get ids from their position, and the buttons of their headers point at them.

A `Toggle` refers to the modal it opens, and the structure writes the button's `data-bs-toggle`, `data-bs-target` and `aria-controls` from the reference.
An `OverlayTrigger` wraps its target in a `span` that carries the tooltip or popover, which works for disabled buttons too.

=== The demo site

The module's `BootstrapDemoTests` generates a static site from the model: a page per package, showing every component, and a gallery of the same showcase in every Bootstrap and Bootswatch theme.
It is written to `target/html-bootstrap/site`; browse it with the JDK's `jwebserver -d target/html-bootstrap/site`.
