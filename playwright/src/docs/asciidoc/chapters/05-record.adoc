== What a walk-through records

=== The model

After the recording is closed, the walk-through is a model, and a test can read it:

.A step, its screenshots and the server's work (`WalkthroughTests.java`)
[source,java]
----
include::{booktests}/WalkthroughTests.java[tag=model,indent=0]
----
<1> The step's log records, in order: its "before" screenshot, its narration, its "after" screenshot. A screenshot's body is the step's name, its path an attribute.
<2> The step's child is the server's span for the search, with the handler's log record.

The test also reduces the trace to an outline of what does not change from run to run, the narration included, and compares it with this file:

.The walk-through in outline (`walkthrough-outline.txt`)
[source,text]
----
include::{testresourcesdir}/walkthrough-outline.txt[]
----

The outline leaves out the requests for the page, its script and its stylesheet, which the browser sends in parallel, and identifiers, timestamps and attributes.
The full record is in `target/walkthrough/traces.yml`: every span with its timing and attributes, every request the server handled, under the step that sent it.

=== Screenshots in the record

A screenshot is a log record with the event name `nasdanika.screenshot`, in the span that was current when it was taken, with these attributes:

[cols="2,4",options="header"]
|===
| Attribute | Value
| `nasdanika.screenshot.name` | The step's name, or the name given to `screenshot`
| `nasdanika.screenshot.path` | The file, relative to the screenshots directory
| `nasdanika.screenshot.phase` | `before`, `after` or `failure` for a step's own, `explicit` for one taken with `screenshot`
| `nasdanika.screenshot.index` | The position in the session, from 1
| `url.full` | The page's URL
|===

The body of the record is the name, so a log viewer that knows nothing of screenshots still shows something readable.

[#narration]
=== Narration

A walk-through can record what a person would say about it: the script of a video, the text of a guide.
`session.narrate(text)` records a line as a log record in the current span, with the event name `nasdanika.narration` and the text as its body, in order with the screenshots; `session.step(name, narration, body)` narrates a step as it starts.

.Narration in a step (`WalkthroughTests.java`)
[source,java]
----
include::{booktests}/WalkthroughTests.java[tag=narration,indent=0]
----
<1> A line of narration, anywhere in a step.
<2> A line with a language, a voice and a pause after it, for speech synthesis and walk-throughs narrated in several languages.
<3> The step's records: the "before" screenshot, the two lines, the "after" screenshot.

The order is what a video or a guide is generated from.
A line of narration is spoken over the last screenshot taken before it, and a line before the first screenshot of a session over the first one: a step narrated as it starts is spoken over its "before" screenshot, while the page still shows what the step is about to change.

[cols="2,4",options="header"]
|===
| Attribute | Value
| `nasdanika.narration.index` | The position in the session, from 1
| `nasdanika.narration.language` | A BCP 47 tag, if given
| `nasdanika.narration.voice` | A voice by the name a renderer knows it, if given
| `nasdanika.narration.pause_ms` | Silence after the line, in milliseconds, if given
| `url.full` | The page's URL
|===

=== The browser's console

Console messages are log records with the event name `nasdanika.browser.console`, the message as the body and the page's URL as an attribute, with a severity by the message's type: `error` is an error, `warning` a warning, `debug` debug, anything else information.
Uncaught errors in the page are log records with the event name `nasdanika.browser.page_error` and the error severity.
They are in the span that was current when Playwright delivered them, usually the step that caused them.
